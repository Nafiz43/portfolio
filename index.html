Nafiz Imtiaz Khan

PhD Student in Computer Science, University of California, Davis

Hi! I am pursuing PhD in Computer Science at University of California - Davis, where I work in DECAL lab with professor Vladimir Filkov. I am co-advised by Professor Roger Eric Goldman. Formerly, I served as a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at MIST.

My research applies large language models, vision-language models, and machine learning to software engineering and health informatics. Recent work covers retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), fine-tuning LLMs for domain-specific tasks, and building scalable ML pipelines for real-world problems.

Apart from my academic activities, I like to explore new things and embrace unknown challenges in my life. In my spare time, I love playing table tennis and badminton.
